MutableNovaRegistry

interface MutableNovaRegistry<T : NovaRegistryElement<T>> : NovaRegistry<T>

A mutable NovaRegistry that allows registering entries and tags via set. After initialization, the registry can be frozen to prevent further modifications and bind values to entries.

Some registries may be reloadable, allowing reload to reconfigure the registered contents of the registry. Corresponding entries and entry sets will be updated accordingly.
