PassengerPacketEntityDsl

sealed interface PassengerPacketEntityDsl<M : EntityMetadataDsl>

DSL scope for configuring a packet entity that is used as a passenger.

Passenger entities do not define their own location, viewer whitelist/blacklist, or spawn/despawn handlers. Instead, they are tied to the parent entity and are spawned and despawned with it.
